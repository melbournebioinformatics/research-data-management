title: Setup

No setup is required for these training materials.

These workshop materials are not meant to replace your institution's set policies and guidance. These recommendations are based on observations and conversations by staff of Melbourne Bioinformatics and meant as general guidance. Always check region-specific, project-specific, and institution-specific guidance and policies before creating your own research data management plan.

For resources and policies related to the University of Melbourne, [please see our dedicated resources page](./10-university-resources.html).
